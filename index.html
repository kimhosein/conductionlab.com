Systems shift people from their baselines.

AI made the shift impossible to ignore — but platforms, algorithms, feeds, and devices have been doing it for years. The evidence is scattered across fields. The research has rarely been connected.

The lab exists to connect it.

What we study

How systems shift people from their baselines — AI, algorithms, platforms, devices. The effects are measurable, and the methodology to measure them already exists in clinical research and pharmacovigilance.

The mechanism

The Conduction Hypothesis: LLMs don't generate human-like signal — they conduct real human signal through training data, producing systematic source misattribution at the point of reception. This changes how we evaluate safety, risk, and readiness.
