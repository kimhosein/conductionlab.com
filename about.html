About

Kim Hosein

Founder of Loopwork System, LLC. Over a decade of Phase I–IV clinical trial experience as a clinical project lead, working inside the systems that track what drugs do to people over time — adverse event reporting, signal detection, causality assessment. The infrastructure that answers: did this exposure cause this change?

That question doesn't stop at pharmaceuticals. People are interacting with AI systems, social platforms, and algorithmically mediated relationships at a scale and intensity that has no precedent. Researchers across disciplines are documenting pieces of what's happening — dose-response effects, longitudinal behavioral change, attachment shifts — but they're working in silos, and no one has applied the clinical signal detection methodology that already exists for exactly this kind of problem.

Loopwork System is the framework that connects those pieces. The same methodology used to determine whether a drug caused an adverse event can detect and measure the baseline shifts caused by any sustained relational exposure. The unit of analysis is the joint distribution — what forms between person and system — not the individual measured in isolation.

Chris Hoyd

Co-developer — Human Behavioral Infrastructure

Chris Hoyd holds a JD and brings over a decade of experience in product strategy, UX design, and user research. His work maps the behavioral substrate that makes baseline shift detection possible — the mechanics of emotional recursion, attachment dynamics, and compulsion patterns that constitute someone's individual behavioral signature. This infrastructure is what allows deviation from the mean to be measured at the individual level rather than lost in group averages.
