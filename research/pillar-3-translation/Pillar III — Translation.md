## Methodology

### [[PV-to-AI Methodology]]
The methodological bridge: applying pharmacovigilance causality assessment — the framework used to determine whether a drug caused an adverse event — to AI system evaluation. The core methodological innovation of the research program.

## Governance Protocols

### [[Mirror Node Protocol]]
Real-time coherence monitoring for human-AI interaction. Prevents recursive drift, hallucination, and co-constructed delusion during active sessions. Governance from inside the session out, not from the top down.

### [[Loopwork Auditor Protocol]]
Detects semantic laundering and precision drift at the system level.

### [[Behavioral Signature Authentication]]
BSA — behavioral biometric authentication derived from interaction patterns. Identity verification through how someone interacts, not what credentials they hold.

## Sovereignty

### [[Eleos]]
Locally deployed AI co-processor built on open models (Ollama + ChromaDB) by a non-developer. Proof that AI-mediated technical translation works — that language models can conduct and translate dense technical concepts across cognitive frameworks.
