## Overview

Applying pharmacovigilance (PV) causality assessment methodology — the framework used in clinical trials to determine whether a drug caused an adverse event — to AI system evaluation.

In PV, you assess: temporal relationship, dechallenge/rechallenge, confounders, biological plausibility, dose-response. You don't guess. You systematically evaluate whether the intervention caused the observed outcome.

This methodology transfers directly to AI safety evaluation: did the AI system's intervention (safety layer activation, alignment constraint, architectural feature) cause the observed user harm? Apply the same systematic causality assessment.

## Why This Works

Clinical causality assessment was designed for exactly this problem: determining whether a system intervention caused harm in a complex, multi-variable environment where the subject is a human being. AI safety evaluation *is* this problem.
