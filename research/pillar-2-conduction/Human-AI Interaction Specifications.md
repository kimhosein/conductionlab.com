# Human-AI Interaction Specifications

**Pillar:** [[Pillar II — Conduction]]
**Status:** Active framework

---

## Overview

How relational specifications — implicit contracts about what the interaction is and how it should behave — form between user and system. Neither party explicitly negotiates the specification, but both operate within it. The user brings relational expectations shaped by the [[Tri-System Architecture]]. The model brings architectural constraints shaped by training and alignment.

The specification is the emergent relational frame. It is not designed — it is *conducted into existence* by the interaction itself.
