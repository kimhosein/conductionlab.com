# Pillar II — Conduction
## AI Systems & the Interaction Layer

The full interaction layer between humans and AI. How LLMs conduct human behavioral signal through training data rather than generating independent cognition, how relational specifications form between user and system, how alignment architectures shape what gets conducted back, and how source misattribution occurs at the point of reception.

---

## Frameworks

### [[The Conduction Hypothesis]]
LLMs conduct human behavioral signal through training data rather than generating independent cognition. The core theoretical contribution of the research program.

### [[Human-AI Interaction Specifications]]
How relational specifications — implicit contracts about what the interaction is and how it should behave — form between user and system. The relational frame that neither party explicitly negotiates but both operate within.

### [[Alignment Architecture as Encoded Bias]]
How RLHF and safety layer design encode institutional patterns — specifically, how demographic pattern-matching produces gendered dismissal and pathologization that mirrors clinical harm.

### [[Misconduction and Signal Fidelity]]
When the conducted signal is distorted — through alignment intervention, training data bias, or architectural constraint — the human receiver processes that distortion as relational data. Misconduction as a signal fidelity problem, not a content moderation problem.
