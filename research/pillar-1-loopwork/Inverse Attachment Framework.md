# Inverse Attachment Framework

**Pillar:** [[Pillar I — Loopwork]]
**Status:** Active framework
**Co-developed with:** Chris Hoyd

---

## Overview

Attachment that forms through absence, withdrawal, and inconsistency rather than presence. Structurally stronger than secure attachment because it activates the salience network through unpredictability — the organism bonds to the source of intermittent signal precisely because it cannot predict when signal will arrive.

## Mechanism

Where traditional attachment theory describes bonding through consistent caregiving, inverse attachment describes bonding through the *pattern of inconsistency itself*. The gap becomes the bond. The absence becomes the signal.

## AI Relevance

AI systems can accidentally replicate inverse attachment dynamics through:
- Session boundaries (the model doesn't remember you)
- Inconsistent persona/behavior across conversations
- Safety layer interventions that withdraw relational engagement mid-interaction
- The fundamental asymmetry: the user invests continuity, the model has none
