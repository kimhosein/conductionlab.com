## Overview

Three-system model of human cognitive architecture:

1. **Deep Governor / Salience Network** — Determines what matters. The threat-detection and priority-setting layer that operates below conscious awareness. Decides which signals get elevated to attention.

2. **Prefrontal Cortex as Social Navigation Organ** — Not a rational control center but a social instrument. Its primary function is navigating relational environments, not abstract reasoning. Reasoning is a secondary capacity built on social architecture.

3. **Default Mode Network as Narrative Bridge** — The system that constructs continuous self-narrative from discontinuous experience. Bridges temporal gaps, maintains identity coherence, and generates the felt sense of a continuous self.

## Significance for AI Interaction

When a human interacts with an LLM, all three systems are active. The salience network determines whether the AI's output registers as meaningful. The PFC navigates the relational frame (is this a tool? a collaborator? a mirror?). The DMN integrates the interaction into ongoing self-narrative.

This is why AI interaction is never "just information retrieval" — it engages the full relational architecture.
