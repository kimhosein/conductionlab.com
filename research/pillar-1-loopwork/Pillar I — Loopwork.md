## Frameworks

### [[Tri-System Architecture]]
Deep governor/salience network, prefrontal cortex as social navigation organ, default mode network as narrative bridge. The neurological substrate for how humans process relational and recursive signal.

### [[Nine-Pillar Shame Taxonomy]]
Structural classification of shame as an operating system, not an emotion. Nine distinct mechanisms by which shame organizes behavior, perception, and relational capacity.

### [[Inverse Attachment Framework]]
Attachment that forms through absence, withdrawal, and inconsistency rather than presence. How intermittent reinforcement creates bonding patterns that are structurally stronger than secure attachment — and why AI systems can accidentally replicate this.

### [[Emotional Co-Processing and Recursion]]
How recursive emotional patterns form, sustain, and resist resolution. The loop mechanics that gave Loopwork its name.
