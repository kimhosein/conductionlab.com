## Overview

How recursive emotional patterns form, sustain, and resist resolution. The loop mechanics that gave Loopwork its name. Recursion is not pathology — it is the system's attempt to process signal that the existing architecture cannot resolve in a single pass.

## Core Concept

Emotional recursion occurs when an experience generates signal that the cognitive system cannot integrate on first processing. The system re-presents the signal (through memory, behavior, relational reenactment) until either the architecture expands to hold it or the loop becomes self-sustaining.
